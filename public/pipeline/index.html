Three sheets, one model

The same house is drawn three times — once by the engineer, once by the architect, once by the electrician. Sixteen passes read all three scans and return a single federated IFC model. Five networks are involved and none of them ever sees another's output; everything they produce is reconciled on the drawing itself.
